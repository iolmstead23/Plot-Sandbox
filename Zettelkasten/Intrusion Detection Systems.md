## What it is
An Intrusion Detection System (IDS) is a security technology designed to monitor [[Networks|network]] or system activities for malicious activities or policy violations. It analyzes [[Networks|network]] traffic or system logs to identify potential intrusions through signature-based or anomaly-based methods.

## How it is used
IDS is deployed by:
- Installing sensors at strategic [[Networks|network]] points (NIDS) or on individual hosts (HIDS)
- Configuring detection rules based on known attack patterns
- Analyzing real-time traffic against these rules
- Generating alerts for suspicious activities
- Integrating with SIEM systems for centralized monitoring

## Why it is important
IDS serves as a critical early warning system that:
- Detects attacks that may bypass firewalls
- Provides visibility into internal threats
- Enables rapid incident response and mitigation
- Helps maintain compliance with security standards
- Supports forensic analysis after an attack occurs

*Note: Unlike firewalls, IDS is primarily defensive and alert-based rather than controlling traffic flow.*
